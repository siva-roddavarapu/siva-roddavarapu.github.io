How the AI features I build find answers.

Scroll through this stage to watch a retrieval run: documents become vectors, a question lands among them, and its nearest neighbours become the context GPT-4 answers from.

illustration · embedding space (2-D)
  1. Embed the documentsEnterprise content is chunked and turned into vectors.
  2. Embed the questionThe user's query becomes a vector in the same space.
  3. Find the nearestSemantic search pulls the top-k closest chunks.
  4. Answer with contextGPT-4 replies grounded in what was retrieved.
